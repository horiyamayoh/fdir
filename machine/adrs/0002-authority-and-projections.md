
# ADR 0002: Canonical authority and generated projections

Status: accepted for FDIR 2.1.0

Canonical source, content-addressed evidence, and rebuildable projections are separate classes. PDF, DOCX, PNG, SVG, SQLite indexes, rendered reports, and binary qualification bundles may aid review or distribution, but their omission from the repository does not remove canonical information. A projection may be published only with source version and digest provenance.
