# Terminology

- **Recorded information**: information asserted to have been recorded, distinct from the physical evidence that supports the assertion.
- **Artifact**: a byte-bearing source or derivative identified by digest and media type.
- **Carrier**: a logical or physical part of an artifact that can contain recorded evidence.
- **Selector**: a machine-actionable locator within a carrier.
- **Occurrence**: an evidenced appearance tied to a carrier and selectors.
- **InformationUnit**: an identity and construction anchor; substantive meaning is expressed through assertions.
- **RecordAssertion**: a status-bearing statement about a unit, supported by occurrences or explicit diagnostics.
- **AcceptedProjection**: a convenience view assembled only from accepted assertions.
- **InventoryDomain**: the bounded population used for completeness accounting.
- **AccountingItem**: the disposition of one source key within an inventory domain.
- **GuaranteeStatus**: the explicit complete, partial, or unavailable status of a profile guarantee.
- **EquivalenceCertificate**: evidence-scoped comparison whose outcome may be equivalent, different, or indeterminate.
- **Canonical identity**: deterministic byte identity; it is separate from unit identity, cross-format equivalence, and cross-revision continuity.
