
# 6. Product claims and qualification

The normative baseline is a design and contract package. It does not contain production converters and does not qualify any format/capability/profile tuple. Schema validity, a passing example, or a demo conversion cannot be reported as production readiness.

A production claim requires a qualification receipt, exact tuple, build provenance, corpus identity, security and resource-limit results, and a release claim manifest. Unsupported and indeterminate outcomes remain visible in user-facing reports.
