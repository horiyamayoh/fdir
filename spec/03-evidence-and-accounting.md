
# 3. Evidence substrate and exhaustive accounting

Artifacts contain bytes. Carriers identify format-specific containers. Selectors locate source material. Occurrences group selectors and optional geometry. Observations record measured or inferred facts without laundering them into authoritative content.

Each independently inventoried source item belongs to one `InventoryDomain` and receives exactly one `AccountingItem` disposition. Represented, residual, unsupported, unreadable, policy-excluded, and duplicate are explicit outcomes. Silent omission is invalid. Profiles may require an `IndependentCensusReceipt` whose observed count closes the domain independently of the adapter's own output.
